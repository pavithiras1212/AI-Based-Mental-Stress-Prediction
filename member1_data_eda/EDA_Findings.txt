8. STRESS-RELATED SYMPTOMS

The dataset contains several reported stress-related symptoms,
including headache, difficulty concentrating, anxiety and poor sleep.

Some combinations involving multiple symptoms showed higher average
stress levels than individual symptoms.

The relationship should be treated as an association rather than
evidence of causation.


9. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

The current correlation matrix contains only the stress-level variable.

Therefore, no meaningful correlation between stress and other
variables can be concluded from the current correlation matrix.

The other survey variables are mainly categorical/text variables and
require suitable encoding before numerical correlation analysis.


10. OVERALL EDA CONCLUSION

The exploratory analysis shows that stress levels vary across
exercise frequency, sleep duration, year of study, age group,
reported causes of stress and stress-related symptoms.

The dataset has 120 student responses and contains no duplicate rows.

The findings provide useful patterns for the next stage of the project,
where relevant variables can be encoded and used as features for
mental stress prediction.

These EDA results describe associations in the survey data and should
not be interpreted as proof that one factor directly causes changes
in stress levels.
